Privacy Policy

Application: Arrow Puzzle  |  Effective Date: August 29, 2026

Summary: Arrow Puzzle is developed by Velocity Games as an offline-first puzzle game. We value your privacy: we do not collect, track, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information to external servers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Velocity Games ("we", "us", or "our") manages user information in connection with our mobile application, Arrow Puzzle (the "Application").

By installing or playing Arrow Puzzle, you agree to the collection and handling of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Arrow Puzzle operates as an offline-first application. We do not require account registration with email or passwords, and we do not collect personal identifiers.

  • Local Profile & Gameplay State: When playing as a guest, your chosen player nickname (GamerTag), avatar selection, stage progress (1–100), star ratings, high scores, and custom workshop levels are stored strictly locally on your device using Android Room Database and DataStore.
  • User Preferences: Game audio toggles (sound effects), haptic vibration preferences, and visual glow settings are saved exclusively on your local device.
  • No Personal Data Harvesting: We do not collect your real name, phone number, location, contacts, device identifiers, or financial information.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

The Application utilizes standard Android device capabilities solely to enhance the in-game experience:

  • Vibration / Haptic Feedback (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile responses during move collisions or stage clearances. This can be toggled on/off at any time in the Game Settings menu.
  • Protected Local Storage: Used strictly to save and load your stage completions and custom created levels within the app's sandboxed storage.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with any third parties. The Application is distributed via the Google Play Store and complies with the Google Play Developer Program Policies.

Standard operating system metrics provided by Google (such as anonymous crash reports or performance telemetry via Google Play Console) are handled in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all game progress and custom stages reside solely on your physical device:

  • You can erase all campaign progress at any time directly within the app by going to Settings > Reset All Stage Progress.
  • Uninstalling the Application or clearing the app data via Android system settings will permanently delete all locally stored progress and preferences.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Puzzle is a family-friendly puzzle game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently processed, please contact us immediately.

7. Security

We take your privacy and data security seriously. All gameplay state and user preferences benefit from the Android operating system's application sandbox and built-in device encryption.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any updates will be posted to this page with an updated "Effective Date".
